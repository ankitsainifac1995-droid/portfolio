About Me & Education

Educator's Profile

I am Ankit Saini, a 31-year-old education professional from Himachal Pradesh, India, with a strong academic foundation in biological sciences and a commitment to excellence in teaching.

My educational journey began at Mount Carmel School, Una. I subsequently completed my Bachelor of Science in Medical Sciences from Himachal Pradesh University, Shimla, followed by a Master's degree in Botany from Panjab University, Chandigarh.

Throughout my professional journey, I have focused on making complex biological concepts accessible, engaging, and relevant. I believe that effective teaching extends beyond textbooks; it involves developing curiosity, critical thinking, scientific understanding, and confidence.
